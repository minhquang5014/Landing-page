Security & trust

Your voice. Your data.
Protected.

We built NextStar with privacy-first principles from day one. Every conversation, message, and piece of personal data is treated with care — never as a product to be sold.

🔐

End-to-End Audio Encryption

All live voice sessions run over LiveKit's secure WebRTC stack with end-to-end encryption. Nobody — not even us — can listen in on your conversations.

🛡️

Row-Level Security

Every database query is enforced by Supabase's Row-Level Security policies. Your data is strictly scoped — other users only see what you explicitly choose to share.

🔑

Signed Room Tokens

Every speaking room requires a cryptographically signed token issued per-session, validated server-side before entry. No spoofing, no uninvited guests.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

We don't sell your data. We don't share transcripts with third parties. Your voice practice is yours — full stop. No exceptions, no asterisks.
