Security & trust

Your voice. Your data. Protected.

We built NextStar with privacy-first principles. Every conversation is treated with care — never as a product to sell.

🔐

End-to-End Audio Encryption

All voice sessions run over LiveKit's secure WebRTC stack. Nobody — not even us — can listen in on your conversations.

🛡️

Row-Level Security

Every database query is enforced by Supabase RLS policies. Your data is strictly scoped — others only see what you share.

🔑

Signed Room Tokens

Every speaking room requires a cryptographically signed token issued per-session. No spoofing, no uninvited guests.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

We don't sell your data. We don't share transcripts with third parties. Your voice practice is yours — full stop.
