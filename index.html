Security & trust

Your voice. Your data. Protected.

We built UniVoice with privacy-first principles. Every conversation, message, and piece of personal data is treated with care — not as a product.

🔐

End-to-End Audio Encryption

All live voice sessions are encrypted in transit using LiveKit's secure WebRTC stack. Nobody — not even us — can listen in on your conversations.

🛡️

Row-Level Security

Every query to our database is governed by Supabase's Row-Level Security. Your data is strictly scoped — other users can only see what you choose to share.

🔑

Signed Room Tokens

Every speaking room requires a cryptographically signed token issued at session time. No spoofing, no uninvited guests — rooms are private by default.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

We don't sell your data. We don't share transcripts with third parties. Your voice practice stays yours — it's as simple as that.
